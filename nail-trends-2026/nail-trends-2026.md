## What is driving 2026

### Elevated simplicity

The year's organising idea. "People want their nails to feel clean, luxurious, and thoughtfully designed without being overly decorated," celebrity artist Kim Truong tells Vogue. Fewer elements, better executed.

### Restraint plus one high-impact detail

Townhouse's Juanita Huber-Millet calls 2026 "elegant restraint"; Queenie Nguyen describes "light-reflective minimalism paired with hyper-textural statement art". Translation: a sheer neutral base and a single sculptural, jewelled or pierced accent.

### Texture beats colour

Tweed, velvet, ribbed glass, chrome and 3D gel replaced last year's flat creams. Chanel under Matthieu Blazy is the reference point - boucle, woven and lace effects translated onto the nail.

### Nail health is the flex

"Healthy nails always start with the natural nail," says Navy Professional founder Rebecca Crawforth. Builder gel/BIAB overlays, cuticle serum and shorter lengths are what make the sheer, milky looks possible.

### Pantone sets the palette

Cloud Dancer, Pantone's 2026 Colour of the Year, is an airy translucent white that anchors the year: it is the base under glitzy French tips, lace art, halo nails and micro dots.

### Maximalism in controlled doses

Allure's "mixed maximalist", ELLE's "glitchy glam" and Who What Wear's "bold and bedazzled" are the counter-current - mismatched hands, asymmetry and charms, usually across two or three accent nails.

## The 30 trends

### Finish & texture

#### 01. Cloud Dancer milky whites

*Pantone's 2026 Colour of the Year, on your nails*

Pantone named Cloud Dancer its 2026 Colour of the Year and the nail world translated it into a translucent, creamy white that reads as a milkshake glow rather than flat white-out. Artists keep it airy - two sheer coats over the natural nail, free edge still visible - and let gloss do the luxury part.

- **Palette/finish:** Sheer creamy off-white (#F4F1EC to #E8E2D9), satin to high gloss
- **Best shape:** Short almond, squoval or short round
- **Peak season:** All year; peaks spring/summer 2026
- **Wearability:** Everyday / office-safe
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Sheer milky white, two thin coats, no visible brushstrokes, high-gloss top coat."
- **Sources:** Good Housekeeping, ELLE UK, Harper's Bazaar UK, Marie Claire (4/11)

#### 02. Ultra-sheer washes

*"Your nails but better" - the polished bare nail*

Vogue lists ultra-sheer washes among its seven biggest trends: a single veil of warm pink, peach or tea-rose tint that evens out the nail bed without hiding it. Sundays Studio describes the effect as "sunshine on water" - warm, natural and flattering across skin tones, which is why it books year-round.

- **Palette/finish:** Tea rose, peach and warm pink veils (#F6E4DE to #E6C3B8), gloss
- **Best shape:** Any; best on short squoval or round
- **Peak season:** Spring/summer 2026, then a year-round staple
- **Wearability:** Everyday / minimal
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "One coat of sheer warm pink, cuticle-clean, glass top coat - nothing opaque."
- **Sources:** Vogue, Harper's Bazaar UK, Marie Claire (spring) (3/11)

#### 03. Juicy glass / jelly glow

*The Korean jelly nail becomes the default neutral*

Syrup-gel colour with a wet, see-through depth that makes nails read as "juicy". Good Housekeeping's experts call it "the best version of a naked nail, but smooth and ultra shiny"; on deeper skin tones it behaves as a glossed tint rather than a colour. ELLE files the same finish under "glassy glow".

- **Palette/finish:** Syrupy rose, peach, cherry-cola, clear overlay (#F5D9CF to #D8A899)
- **Best shape:** Short oval, squoval, medium almond
- **Peak season:** Spring/summer; returns as syrup browns in autumn
- **Wearability:** Everyday to evening
- **Difficulty:** 2/5
- **Ask for:** "Jelly/syrup gel in a translucent rose, three thin coats, high-build gloss top."
- **Sources:** Good Housekeeping, ELLE UK, Marie Claire (3/11)

#### 04. Soft chrome & pearl glaze

*Chrome loses the mirror, gains the glow*

Harper's Bazaar's Juanita Huber-Millet describes 2026 chrome as "whisper-thin metallic washes over nudes, or pearlescent sheens layered over warm browns... less mirror shine, more soft luminosity". It is the glazed-doughnut finish matured into something quieter and more expensive-looking.

- **Palette/finish:** Pearl, champagne and soft silver washes (#DCD9D2 to #EFE9DF)
- **Best shape:** Almond, oval or squoval; short works too
- **Peak season:** All year; peaks in party season
- **Wearability:** Everyday to occasion
- **Difficulty:** 2/5
- **Ask for:** "Pearl chrome powder over a warm nude - keep it a soft sheen, not mirror."
- **Sources:** Harper's Bazaar UK, ELLE UK, Allure (fall colour) (3/11)

#### 05. Velvet chrome

*Cat-eye velvet with a chrome overlay*

Two 2026 favourites collided. Vogue predicts velvet nails finished with a cat-eye magnetic pull and a chrome overlay, giving a soft brush-stroke shimmer that shifts as the hand moves - all the texture of glitter with none of the grit. ILNP's Ritual and Kiss's Fine n Cool press-ons are the high-street route in.

- **Palette/finish:** Dusty mauve, gunmetal and soft navy (#6E6A78 to #B9B2C4)
- **Best shape:** Medium almond or oval - velvet reads best on longer beds
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026
- **Wearability:** Evening / statement
- **Difficulty:** 3/5
- **Ask for:** "Velvet cat-eye gel with a chrome topper, magnetic pull front to back."
- **Sources:** Vogue, Good Housekeeping, ELLE UK (3/11)

#### 06. Cat-eye magnetic 3D

*Pigment pulled into halos, bands and domes*

Magnetic pigment is pulled with a magnet into a band, a halo or a 3D dome. Artist Emi Kudo layers Apres Astral Gels over a dark base (Midnight Shadow) for a shimmer that looks lit from inside; Good Housekeeping notes the effect is now routinely combined with charms or a chrome overlay.

- **Palette/finish:** Ink, plum, bronze or emerald bases with magnetic shimmer
- **Best shape:** Almond, coffin, oval
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026, holiday
- **Wearability:** Evening / statement
- **Difficulty:** 3/5
- **Ask for:** "Cat-eye gel over a dark base, magnetic halo effect, no craft glitter."
- **Sources:** Good Housekeeping, Vogue, ELLE UK (3/11)

#### 07. Molten & mirror metallics

*Liquid metal, worn as jewellery*

Full-coverage liquid metal - silver, gold, bronze - was the loudest finish of the fall 2026 shows and feeds the celestial trend. Allure's fall colour report names molten silver the season's "treat yourself" shade: it reads as jewellery on short nails and as armour on long almond or coffin tips.

- **Palette/finish:** Molten silver, antique gold, bronze (#C8C9CC, #D4B878, #A9744F)
- **Best shape:** Short squoval, or long almond/coffin
- **Peak season:** Fall/winter 2026, holiday
- **Wearability:** Occasion / party
- **Difficulty:** 2/5
- **Ask for:** "Full-coverage chrome or metallic silver, no glitter particles, mirror top coat."
- **Sources:** Allure (fall colour), Good Housekeeping, ELLE UK (3/11)

#### 08. Blown & ribbed glass

*The glass nail, sculpted instead of just shiny*

Allure's "blown glass" and Good Housekeeping's "ribbed glass" push the glass nail into three dimensions: sculpted bubbles, fine ribs and holographic layers sealed under a clear dome. The spring 2026 runways did the same thing at Head of State, where nails looked literally glass-blown.

- **Palette/finish:** Clear, icy blue and pearl with holographic shifts
- **Best shape:** Short-to-medium almond; sculpture needs length
- **Peak season:** Spring/summer 2026
- **Wearability:** Statement / editorial
- **Difficulty:** 5/5
- **Ask for:** "Clear builder gel sculpted into ribs or bubbles, holographic flake, high dome."
- **Sources:** Allure, Good Housekeeping, Harper's Bazaar (runway) (3/11)

### Colour

#### 09. Comfort neutrals

*Caramel, chai and butter beige*

Harper's Bazaar calls them "comfort colours": caramel, chai latte, buttery beige and warm mocha. Worn sheer-to-medium, these are what clients ask for when they want something cosier than a nude but safer than a colour - the palette equivalent of a cashmere throw.

- **Palette/finish:** Caramel, chai, butter beige, mocha (#C8A279 to #E0C9AE)
- **Best shape:** Any; almond and short squoval lead
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026
- **Wearability:** Everyday / office
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Warm caramel nude, sheer-to-medium coverage, gloss."
- **Sources:** Harper's Bazaar UK, Marie Claire (2/11)

#### 10. Cool-toned browns / espresso

*Mocha cools down and goes deeper*

The mocha of 2024-25 has cooled and deepened. Good Housekeeping flags cool-toned browns; Allure's fall 2026 report leads with "cozy browns" such as JinSoon Earth Clay (sent down Proenza Schouler) and Essie Never Skip a Beat, plus red-browns like Orly Canyon Clay. Marie Claire's autumn story is the same coffee mood.

- **Palette/finish:** Cocoa, espresso, taupe-brown (#6B4B3A to #4A322A)
- **Best shape:** Medium almond or square
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026
- **Wearability:** Everyday to evening
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Cool espresso brown, opaque, satin or gloss."
- **Sources:** Good Housekeeping, Allure (fall colour), Marie Claire (3/11)

#### 11. Matured reds

*Brick, bordeaux and cherry instead of bright tomato*

Red, but grown up. Harper's Bazaar calls it "matured reds" - brick, wine and Bordeaux - while Allure's fall cherry is deeper still: Hermes Rouge H, Dazzle Dry Fast-Track Cherry and Nailboo's cola-toned Cola Kiss. Opaque, high-shine and unadorned; the colour is the whole design.

- **Palette/finish:** Brick, bordeaux, merlot, cherry (#7E1F26 to #5C1218)
- **Best shape:** Short almond or squoval; classic on oval
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026 (plus Valentine's)
- **Wearability:** Everyday to evening
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Deep bordeaux red, opaque, high-gloss - no shimmer."
- **Sources:** Harper's Bazaar UK, Allure (fall colour), Marie Claire (3/11)

#### 12. Sapphire, electric & aqua blues

*Jewel-box sapphire meets bright cobalt*

Blue split into two moods: the sapphire of Allure's fall 2026 report (shades such as Sapphire Season and Playing With Sapphire) and the electric and aquamarine blues of spring. Good Housekeeping lists electric blue among the year's most-requested colours; ELLE's "cool blue" layers it under a silver chrome topper.

- **Palette/finish:** Sapphire, electric cobalt, icy aquamarine (#1F3A6E, #2F6FB5, #7FC7D9)
- **Best shape:** Short square or squoval for brights; almond for sapphire
- **Peak season:** Summer 2026 brights; winter 2026 sapphire
- **Wearability:** Statement / holiday
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Opaque sapphire (or electric cobalt), gloss; chrome topper optional."
- **Sources:** Good Housekeeping, ELLE UK, Allure (fall colour), Marie Claire (spring) (4/11)

#### 13. Earthy greens

*Olive, moss and chartreuse*

Olive, moss and chartreuse moved from the runway to the fingers. Allure's fall 2026 greens - Lights Lacquer Extra Dirty, Gel Jukebox Olive, The Stranger - are muddy and wearable; Marie Claire puts chartreuse at the loud end of the same family. Matte top coat makes them read modern rather than retro.

- **Palette/finish:** Olive, moss, chartreuse (#6B6B3A, #4E5B31, #A8B36A)
- **Best shape:** Short squoval; chartreuse on almond
- **Peak season:** Autumn 2026
- **Wearability:** Everyday to statement
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Muddy olive green, opaque, matte or satin top coat."
- **Sources:** Allure (fall colour), Marie Claire (2/11)

#### 14. Not-quite-black

*Black that reveals its colour in daylight*

The compromise between black and colour: near-black plum, midnight navy and oxblood that read black indoors and show their hue in sunlight. Allure's fall list bundles Essie Wicked, OPI Lincoln Park After Dark and Manucurist Hollyhock; the winter pedicure story pushes the same idea into multichrome.

- **Palette/finish:** Near-black plum, midnight navy, oxblood (#1A1418 to #2B1E26)
- **Best shape:** Short oval, almond, square
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026
- **Wearability:** Evening / everyday for the bold
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Near-black plum, two coats, gloss - dark but not flat black."
- **Sources:** Allure (fall colour), Marie Claire (2/11)

#### 15. Cloudy pastels

*Lilac, butter yellow and peach blossom*

The "Goldilocks" shades Allure describes as neither summery-bright nor wintry-dark. Lilac was fall 2026's surprise, butter yellow keeps rolling through 2026 and Marie Claire's spring list adds peach blossom and powder pink - all best in a cloudy, milky finish rather than a flat creme.

- **Palette/finish:** Lilac, butter yellow, peach blossom, powder pink (#C9B8D8, #F3E3A8, #F6D3C4)
- **Best shape:** Short almond or squoval
- **Peak season:** Spring 2026 into early autumn
- **Wearability:** Everyday
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Cloudy lilac (or butter yellow) - sheer and milky, never neon."
- **Sources:** Allure (fall colour), Marie Claire (spring) (2/11)

### Nail art

#### 16. Stripes & athletic-sock tips

*2026's successor to the polka dot*

Allure's number-one art trend: pinstripes, barcode stripes, awning stripes and the "athletic sock" tip Miss Pop painted at Alice + Olivia spring/summer 2026 - two thin horizontal lines across a bare or white tip. Good Housekeeping's retro striped French stacks colour bands instead of one solid tip.

- **Palette/finish:** Contrast pairs: red/white, black/cream, navy/pink
- **Best shape:** Square or squoval - stripes need a straight tip
- **Peak season:** Spring/summer 2026; sports-luxe all year
- **Wearability:** Statement / everyday
- **Difficulty:** 3/5
- **Ask for:** "Two thin horizontal stripes across the tip, crisp edges, gloss."
- **Sources:** Allure, Good Housekeeping, Harper's Bazaar (runway) (3/11)

#### 17. Micro polka dots

*The dot shrinks to 1-2 mm*

After 2025's full-size dots, 2026 does them micro: one or two millimetre spots in a scattered or grid layout, or a single dot per nail. ELLE names micro polka dots among its eight biggest trends, and Good Housekeeping points out a toothpick is enough to DIY the whole set.

- **Palette/finish:** Monochrome, tonal nudes, or one accent colour on Cloud Dancer white
- **Best shape:** Short round or squoval
- **Peak season:** Spring/summer 2026
- **Wearability:** Everyday
- **Difficulty:** 2/5
- **Ask for:** "Micro dots, 1-2 mm, evenly spaced, on a sheer milky base."
- **Sources:** Good Housekeeping, ELLE UK, Marie Claire (spring) (3/11)

#### 18. Glitzy French

*The French tip, set with stones*

The French tip got jewellery: crystal-studded tips, silver studs and rhinestone rows over a crisp neutral base. "It's like jewelry for your nails," artist Sofi Mazur tells Allure; Valeriia Telemaniuk recommends a proper crystal adhesive rather than sticking gems onto wet gel, which "will pop off in two days".

- **Palette/finish:** Nude base plus clear crystal, silver or multicolour rhinestones
- **Best shape:** Almond or square; short French also works
- **Peak season:** Holiday 2026, weddings, party season
- **Wearability:** Occasion
- **Difficulty:** 4/5
- **Ask for:** "Slim French tip covered in small crystals, set with crystal adhesive, no bulky gel."
- **Sources:** Allure, Harper's Bazaar UK (2/11)

#### 19. Soft, slim & milky French

*Diffused tips, tonal instead of white-on-pink*

The French softened: ultra-slim tips, diffused edges and tonal pairs - milky base with an off-white tip, or chocolate over caramel. Who What Wear's "cloudy French" and Good Housekeeping's "milky French" describe the same airbrushed edge, and it is the most wearable nail-art trend of the year.

- **Palette/finish:** Milky base + off-white tip; caramel + chocolate; sheer pink + white
- **Best shape:** Short almond, oval or squoval
- **Peak season:** All year; peaks spring/summer
- **Wearability:** Everyday / bridal / office
- **Difficulty:** 2/5
- **Ask for:** "Soft diffused French, very thin tip, off-white on milky, no harsh line."
- **Sources:** Harper's Bazaar UK, Good Housekeeping, Who What Wear, Allure (4/11)

#### 20. Preppy plaid & argyle

*Ralph Lauren energy, painted on*

Vogue's prep-school plaid and Good Housekeeping's argyle ride the argyle-knit revival: jelly plaid, diamonds with fine criss-cross lines, or dark-academia colourways built from a jewel base. Kylie Jenner's jelly plaid by Zola Ganzorigt is the reference image clients bring to the salon.

- **Palette/finish:** Jewel tones, burgundy, forest, cream with fine white lines
- **Best shape:** Square, squoval or short almond
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026, peak December
- **Wearability:** Statement / seasonal
- **Difficulty:** 4/5
- **Ask for:** "Plaid or argyle: jelly base, thin white criss-cross lines, one accent diamond."
- **Sources:** Vogue, Good Housekeeping (2/11)

#### 21. Textile textures: tweed, lace & silk

*Runway fabric becomes a nail technique*

Chanel under Matthieu Blazy is the reference: tweed, woven texture, lace and silk translated onto the nail. Vogue lists both Chanel tweed and lace-and-silk among its seven trends; Who What Wear's lace art wears the same idea as an accent nail or under a French tip, with Cloud Dancer as the base.

- **Palette/finish:** Boucle cream, tweed multi-tones, black lace over Cloud Dancer
- **Best shape:** Short almond or oval - texture needs a small canvas
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026, couture mood
- **Wearability:** Editorial / occasion
- **Difficulty:** 5/5
- **Ask for:** "Hand-painted lace (or tweed) on one accent nail, matte top coat to fake fabric."
- **Sources:** Vogue, Who What Wear (2/11)

#### 22. Celestial & halo nails

*Moon dust, crystals and a ring of colour*

Two star-themed looks dominate. "Extra celestial" - silver metallic, crystals, 3D charms and hardware that reads outer space - was flagged by Pinterest for 2026; halo nails draw a ring of polish around the nail edge instead of a tip (Dua Lipa wore them). Both work as one accent nail or a full set.

- **Palette/finish:** Moon-dust silver and pearl; halos in contrast brights
- **Best shape:** Almond and oval for halo; any for celestial
- **Peak season:** Winter 2026 / party season
- **Wearability:** Statement / evening
- **Difficulty:** 3/5
- **Ask for:** "Silver chrome with a few crystals at the cuticle, or a 2 mm halo outline."
- **Sources:** Who What Wear, ELLE UK, Marie Claire (3/11)

#### 23. Nail piercing & hardware

*Rings, studs and bangles, worn on the nail*

Actual jewellery: tiny rings, bars and studs pierced through the free edge or set into gel, plus Good Housekeeping's "bangles nails" - stacked gold, silver or rose-gold bands with gemstones. Allure pairs black polish with "a piercing (or five)". The look is punk rather than precious.

- **Palette/finish:** Black, cherry or nude base plus gold/silver hardware
- **Best shape:** Square or almond with enough free edge to pierce
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026
- **Wearability:** Statement / editorial
- **Difficulty:** 5/5
- **Ask for:** "One gold ring through the tip plus a stud at the base; drill and seal properly."
- **Sources:** Allure, Good Housekeeping (2/11)

#### 24. Sculptural 3D accents

*3D, but quiet - pearls and soft gel swirls*

Three dimensions, restrained: micro pearls, delicate studs, soft gel swirls and negative space instead of 2025's charm-heavy maximalism. Harper's Bazaar calls it "sculpted minimalism"; Good Housekeeping notes 3D gel swirls coated in chrome powder for a harder, more graphic finish.

- **Palette/finish:** Tonal nudes with pearl, chrome or gold accents
- **Best shape:** Short almond or squoval; keep sculpture to 1-2 nails
- **Peak season:** All year; wedding-heavy in summer
- **Wearability:** Occasion / subtle statement
- **Difficulty:** 4/5
- **Ask for:** "One sculpted pearl or chrome swirl per hand over a sheer nude, negative space."
- **Sources:** Harper's Bazaar UK, Vogue, Good Housekeeping, Who What Wear (4/11)

#### 25. Animal print & bambicore

*Leopard, deer print and horse motifs*

Leopard, deer print and horse motifs - Good Housekeeping labels the trend "bambicore" - replace last year's tortoiseshell. Vogue's Chanel reference pushes a refined leopard in muted browns, and Marie Claire's October 2026 report confirms animal print as the season's art story. Keep it tonal so it reads expensive rather than fancy-dress.

- **Palette/finish:** Camel, cocoa, cream and black spots
- **Best shape:** Almond, oval or short square
- **Peak season:** Autumn/winter 2026
- **Wearability:** Statement
- **Difficulty:** 4/5
- **Ask for:** "Tonal leopard on two or three nails, brown on cream, tiny spots, matte top."
- **Sources:** Good Housekeeping, Vogue, Marie Claire (3/11)

#### 26. Maximalist mix & glitchy glam

*Mismatched hands, on purpose*

The counter-trend: mismatched hands, clashing colours, matte against shiny 3D, Y2K references. Allure's "mixed maximalist" and ELLE's "glitchy glam" describe the same instinct - deliberate asymmetry after years of clean-girl minimalism - and it is the easiest trend to DIY because there are no rules.

- **Palette/finish:** Anything: one matte, one gloss and one metallic per set
- **Best shape:** Mixed shapes across hands (short plus medium)
- **Peak season:** Summer 2026 festival season
- **Wearability:** Statement
- **Difficulty:** 4/5
- **Ask for:** "Mismatched set: one hand matte brights, one hand gloss nudes, same accent charm."
- **Sources:** Allure, ELLE UK, Who What Wear (3/11)

### Shape

#### 27. Sculpted almond & elongated oval

*Shape is the hero, colour is the support*

The salon default for 2026. Harper's Bazaar's experts say shape is now "the hero, with colour and art as supporting details"; Vogue lists sculpted almond among its seven trends, and Pure Nails' 2026 shape report still puts almond as the single most-requested shape because it lengthens the hand without stiletto maintenance.

- **Palette/finish:** Works with every finish; chrome and velvet look best
- **Best shape:** Sculpted almond / elongated oval, medium length
- **Peak season:** All year
- **Wearability:** Everyday to bridal
- **Difficulty:** 2/5
- **Ask for:** "Medium almond, apex at the stress point, tapered but not pointed."
- **Sources:** Harper's Bazaar UK, Vogue, Good Housekeeping, Pure Nails (4/11)

#### 28. Soft square

*The square comeback, with rounded corners*

The surprise of the spring 2026 runways: square tips returned, but soft - gently rounded corners rather than the sharp 2000s block. Harper's Bazaar's runway report leads with square as spring's biggest shape story and notes extra-long coffin as its exaggerated cousin; Good Housekeeping lists square among the year's key shapes.

- **Palette/finish:** Best with brights, stripes and French tips
- **Best shape:** Soft square, short to medium
- **Peak season:** Spring/summer 2026, then all year
- **Wearability:** Everyday
- **Difficulty:** 2/5
- **Ask for:** "Soft square - straight sides, corners filed round, medium length."
- **Sources:** Harper's Bazaar (runway), Good Housekeeping, Pure Nails (3/11)

#### 29. Squoval & short-and-sweet

*The practical winner of 2026*

Squoval is strong like a square, soft like an oval and the least likely to snag. ELLE calls it the "Squoval Revival"; Harper's Bazaar's "short and sweet" pairs it with sheer, milky colour; Pure Nails rates it the easiest shape to maintain and among the strongest - which is why it is the default for anyone with an active life.

- **Palette/finish:** Sheers, milky whites, soft chromes
- **Best shape:** Squoval / short oval
- **Peak season:** All year
- **Wearability:** Everyday / office / active
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ask for:** "Short squoval, rounded corners, sheer milky colour, glass top coat."
- **Sources:** ELLE UK, Harper's Bazaar UK, Pure Nails, Good Housekeeping (4/11)

#### 30. Coffin & the cat-claw curve

*The statement shape at the far end*

Extra-long coffin appeared at Knwls, and Naomi Yasuda built a curved, feline "cat-claw" shape for L'Enchanteur - a dramatic C-curve that follows the finger. Both are extension territory, and the shapes that carry chrome, molten metallics and 3D sculpture best.

- **Palette/finish:** Chrome, molten metallic, 3D embellishment
- **Best shape:** Coffin or cat-claw (curved)
- **Peak season:** Runway-led; holiday 2026
- **Wearability:** Editorial / statement
- **Difficulty:** 5/5
- **Ask for:** "Long coffin with a deep C-curve, or a cat-claw taper; expect extensions."
- **Sources:** Harper's Bazaar (runway), Pure Nails (2/11)

## Method

Editorial round-ups published 17 Dec 2025 - 25 Aug 2026 were read in full, their trend lists de-duplicated, and overlapping names merged into one entry (for example "cloudy French", "milky French" and "the soft, slim French"). The consensus figure counts how many of the sources name the look. Colour chips and hex values in the HTML version are approximations, not exact polish shades; prices and availability were not verified. Quoted statements are attributed to the named expert in the source.
